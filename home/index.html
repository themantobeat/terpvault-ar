Ten kings, standing

Each king size pre-roll drops into its own tapered well, upright and apart. No crushed tips.

Two seals

A flat ring sits on the shoulder and a second ring sits inside the cap. Two seals preserving freshness.

Twist it shut

The triple-start thread pulls the cap down in a short twist, pressing both seal rings into place.
